dried fenugreek leaves

Dried fenugreek leaves, or kasoori methi, as well as the fresh variety, are a fragrant herb frequently used in Indian cuisine. Fresh leaves are hard to come by in some countries, but dried leaves are sold in some stores. Fenugreek has a unique aroma that resembles maple syrup.
